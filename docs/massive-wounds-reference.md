# Candlelight - Massive Wounds Reference

## General Rules

- Massive Wounds use the Hit Location of the triggering damage.
- Location roll: Legs 1-2; Arms 3-4; Torso 5-9; Head 10.
- Resolve the final severity and every lower severity for that location.
- Resolve from highest severity downward. A higher-severity version of the same effect supersedes lower versions.
- If a higher severity automatically inflicts an effect that a lower severity allows a Test to avoid, skip the lower Test.
- Distinct effects remain cumulative.
- For escalating Infection, gain only the highest applicable amount.
- Reaching 0 HP or lower automatically causes a Massive Wound with no prevention Test.
- Characters at 0 HP or below can otherwise act normally unless another effect prevents it.
- Healing can restore a character from negative HP to positive HP without additional penalty. Healing does not remove Massive Wounds unless stated.
- Resolved severity 10+ causes immediate death; do not resolve lower results.
- Candlelight always rounds down.

### Hand Injury
Attacks and Parry Challenge Rolls involving the injured hand are Disheartened. After committing to a Cast, each injured hand contributes a 50% chance for the Cast to fail. One injured hand is 50%; two injured hands cause automatic failure.

### Unusable Arm
An unusable Arm cannot hold or wield items, attack, Block, Parry, or satisfy an Ability or spell requirement requiring that Arm. Losing the Arm supersedes Hand Injury and the unusable-Arm state for that Arm.

### Missing Legs
Losing one Leg reduces Agility by half, rounding down. Losing both Legs sets Agility to 0. This persists while the limb is missing. Limb loss supersedes Hindered and the inability-to-stand injury associated with that Leg.

## Keywords Used Here

- **Disheartened:** Make the affected Roll twice and use the worse result.
- **Hindered:** Total Movement is halved, rounding down.
- **Infection:** A stacking effect. Infection damage is separate and must be explicitly named.
- **Medical Attention:** Treatment associated with the Medicine Skill; exact procedure TBD.
- **Silenced:** Cannot speak or Cast spells.
- **Suffocating:** Progressive Vigor-based condition that can lead to death; full progression TBD.
- **Unconscious:** Cannot act.
- **Vulnerable (Type):** After all other ordinary damage modifiers, increase applicable damage by 50%, immediately before Final Damage modifiers. Vulnerable (All) applies to all Damage Types.
- **Final Damage:** Damage after all ordinary offensive modifiers, including Resistant/Vulnerable, and before the damage is passed to the Target for DR and other defensive effects. Effects explicitly modifying Final Damage resolve last on the offensive side.
